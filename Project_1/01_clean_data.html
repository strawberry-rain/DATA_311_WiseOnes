Purpose of this document

This pipeline is designed following Veredical Data Science (VDS) principles to standardize, clean, and validate messy campus datasets (the Commuter Survey and Population Addresses) into high-trust artifacts without modifying raw source files.

Step 1 — Domain / observational units

  • Synthetic Commuter Survey:
    • Observational unit: One row represents one campus commuter’s annual survey response.
    • Primary Key: A unique combination of respondent_id and standardized academic year.
    • Domain Expectation: Percentages under mode columns (pct_*) must sum up exactly to 100%. Derived trips_per_week should be calculated as 2 * days_commuting if missing. mode_primary must correspond to the mode with the highest percentage share.
  • Synthetic Campus Population Addresses:
    • Observational unit: One row represents one unique campus affiliate.
    • Primary Key: pop_id.
    • Domain Expectation: Addresses must be geocodable. Roles and states must be standardized to pre-defined categories (Student, Faculty, Staff) and 'CA'.

Step 5 — Summary change log & metrics

Explanation: Row Count Completeness

Under standard VDS conventions, cleaning does not mean discarding records. In-place data cleaning and standardisation are executed to preserve complete survey sets (7,129 rows) and administrative population files (7,785 rows) without leaking sample size.

Explanation: Address Sampling Design

To respect OpenStreetMap’s terms of service, geocoding validation was restricted to a random subset sample of 50 addresses. This displays sufficient validation proof without causing service disruptions.
